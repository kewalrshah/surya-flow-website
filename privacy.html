Privacy Policy

Surya Flow does not require an account and includes no advertising, tracking or analytics.

Last Updated 6 November 2026

The short version

Surya Flow does not collect any personal data. The developer cannot see your practice history, your settings or anything you do in the app.

On your device

Your practice history and settings are saved on your device. Daily reminders are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server.

Removing the app removes its data from your device; device backups may keep copies.

iCloud Backup

Your practice history and settings are automatically kept in your own private iCloud account, so they come back if you reinstall the app and stay the same on your devices. The developer cannot see them.

To stop this, or to delete what’s stored, turn off or manage Surya Flow under iCloud in your device’s Settings.

Apple Health

If you turn it on in Settings, Surya Flow saves each practice to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes, a yoga workout or both, depending on what you allow.

It reads nothing from Apple Health, and you can change what it may save at any time in the Health app. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Works offline

Practising works completely offline. The only connection Surya Flow makes is to your own iCloud account, for iCloud Backup.

Notifications

If you set a daily reminder, Surya Flow asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are created on your device and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Feedback and contact

Send Feedback opens your email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Your email address and anything you include are shared with the developer and email providers to handle your feedback. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Emails are used only to reply to you and improve the app, and are never sold or shared for marketing.

Children

Surya Flow is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Wellbeing guidance

Surya Flow provides general education. It is not a substitute for medical advice or professional instruction. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or are recovering from an injury, check with a doctor before you begin.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
