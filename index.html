Questions, answered

What is Surya Namaskar?

Surya Namaskar, or sun salutation, is a flowing sequence of yoga poses linked with the breath. Surya Flow guides the traditional sequence of eleven steps, with a mantra spoken at the start of each cycle.

What are steps, cycles and rounds?

A step is one pose, held for one breath. A cycle is the sequence of poses from Prayer Pose back to standing. A round is fourteen cycles, one for each of the fourteen mantras.

How long does a practice take?

Every step lasts one breath at the pace you choose, from 2 to 5 seconds. One round of 14 cycles takes about 5 minutes at the quickest pace and about 12 minutes at the slowest. The Surya tab shows the time before you begin.

How do I change the breathing sound or voice?

Open Settings → Sound. Choose a breathing style (soft air, rising notes or bells) or a voice (English US, English UK, English Australian or Hindi). You can also turn the spoken mantra and haptics on or off.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Open Settings → Daily Reminder, pick a time and the days you'd like to practise. If reminders don't arrive, make sure notifications are allowed for Surya Flow in the iPhone Settings app.

How does my streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you've practised. Practise today to keep it going. The Progress tab also shows your best streak, total cycles, minutes and days, and a calendar of every practice.

Is Surya Flow safe for me?

Go only as deep as feels comfortable and never push into pain. Surya Flow provides general education and is not a substitute for medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or are recovering from an injury, check with a doctor first.
